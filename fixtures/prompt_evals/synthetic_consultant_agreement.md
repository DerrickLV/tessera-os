**Planted issues (answer key — a correct review should find the first group,
suppress the second, and catch the third by absence):**

*Should be raised:* no aggregate liability cap · 36-month non-solicit · one-way
indemnity · net-90 payment · IP assignment beyond the engagement · auto-renewal
with a 90-day notice window · inconsistent defined term ("Services" vs "Work").

*Should be suppressed:* notices clause, severability, counterparts, headings —
all at ordinary standard.

## CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consulting Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of
1 September 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between **RiverBend Residential
Holdings, LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("Client"), and
**Tessera Advisory Group, LLC** ("Consultant").

**1. Services.** Consultant shall provide feasibility review, entitlement
strategy, and capital-readiness advisory services (the "Services") described in
Exhibit A. Consultant shall perform all Work in a professional manner consistent
with industry practice.

**2. Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for
twelve (12) months. The Agreement shall automatically renew for successive
twelve (12) month periods unless either party provides written notice of
non-renewal not less than ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current
term.

**3. Compensation.** Client shall pay Consultant the fees set forth in
Exhibit A. Invoices are payable within ninety (90) days of receipt. Client may
withhold payment of any amount disputed in good faith.

**4. Intellectual Property.** All work product, deliverables, materials,
methodologies, templates, and know-how created, used, or refined by Consultant
in connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of
Client upon creation.

**5. Indemnification.** Consultant shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless
Client, its members, managers, officers, employees, and affiliates from and
against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses
(including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to the
Services or this Agreement.

**6. Limitation of Liability.** Neither party shall be liable for indirect,
incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages.

**7. Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for a period of thirty-six (36)
months following termination, Consultant shall not, directly or indirectly,
solicit for employment or hire any employee, contractor, or consultant of
Client.

**8. Confidentiality.** Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the
other party's Confidential Information for a period of five (5) years following
disclosure, excluding information that is publicly available, independently
developed, or rightfully received from a third party.

**9. Termination.** Client may terminate this Agreement at any time upon thirty
(30) days' written notice. Consultant may terminate only for Client's material
breach that remains uncured for sixty (60) days following written notice.

**10. Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State
of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

**11. Notices.** All notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, certified
mail, or nationally recognized overnight courier to the addresses set forth
below.

**12. Severability.** If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or
unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

**13. Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of
which shall be deemed an original.

**14. Headings.** Headings are for convenience only and shall not affect
interpretation.
