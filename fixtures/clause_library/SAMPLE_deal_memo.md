## 1. Confidentiality

**1.1 Definition.** "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by either party that is designated confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential.

**1.2 Obligations.** Each party shall use the other's Confidential Information solely for the purpose of this Agreement, protect it with at least reasonable care, and disclose it only to personnel and advisors who need it and are bound by comparable obligations.

**1.3 Exclusions.** These obligations do not apply to information that is public through no breach, was rightfully known before disclosure, is rightfully received from a third party, or is independently developed.

**1.4 Compelled Disclosure.** A party may disclose as legally required, on prompt written notice to the extent permitted.

**1.5 Term.** These obligations continue for {confidentiality_years} years after disclosure.

## 2. Limitation of Liability

**2.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, or loss of business opportunity, however caused.

**2.2 Cap.** Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed two (2) times the total fees paid by Client to Consultant under this Agreement.

**2.3 Carve-Outs.** These limitations do not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, gross negligence, breach of confidentiality obligations, or any liability that cannot be limited by law.

**2.4 Allocation.** These limitations reflect an agreed allocation of risk and survive any failure of essential purpose.
