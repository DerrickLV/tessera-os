# Structure Diagnosis — existing agreement

> A deterministic screen of the document's text against the position set: what it addresses, what it lacks, and what the gaps expose. Detection is not endorsement — language that is present may still be inadequate, and only qualified counsel can say. Not legal advice.

## Missing, and required

The document does not appear to address these at all. Each entry states what goes wrong without it.

**Authority.** The draft would not distinguish routine member authority from jointly controlled decisions.

**Buyout Payment.** A buy-out the buyer cannot fund is not a remedy. Without payment terms, every purchase right in the agreement is exercisable only by whoever happens to hold cash.

**Buysell.** Without a buy-sell procedure, a triggering event creates a right to buy with no mechanism to exercise it, no price, and no closing.

**Capital Default.** A funding request without an agreed process can leave the venture unable to meet an approved obligation.

**Confidentiality.** Without carve-outs, an ordinary compelled disclosure becomes a breach.

**Duties.** Default statutory duties may be broader or narrower than the members intend; silence leaves the question to litigation.

**Exit.** Without a stated liquidation waterfall, distribution on wind-up defaults to the statute, which may not match the members' economic deal.

**Information.** Without stated reporting, a passive member learns about a problem after it is unfixable.

**Restrictive Covenants.** The draft would not identify what confidential information remains protected after a Member leaves.

**Tax Distributions.** Pass-through income is taxed to the member whether or not cash was distributed. Without a tax distribution, a profitable year can hand an owner a tax bill and no money to pay it.

**Titles.** Titles get handed out casually in founder-led businesses and then get relied on. Without this, a counterparty can argue that a person carrying a title had authority the agreement never granted, and a member can argue the title carried economics.

**Transfer.** Without transfer restrictions, a member can sell to anyone — including a competitor or a party who fails a regulatory suitability test.

**Triggering Events.** Without triggering events, death, disability, divorce, bankruptcy, or a member simply walking away leaves that member's full economic and voting rights intact, in the hands of whoever now holds them.

**Valuation.** A buy-out right with no valuation method is a lawsuit with extra steps. The parties reach the moment they most need a number and have no agreed way to produce one.

**Work Product.** In a business whose value is how it thinks, the models, frameworks, and templates are the enterprise value. Without an assignment they belong to whoever wrote them and leave when that person does.

## Missing, and conditional

Not every agreement needs these; whether this one does is a judgment call the screen cannot make.

**Deadlock.** Equal owners may have no agreed escalation path for an unresolved reserved decision.

**Estate Transfer.** A routine estate-planning transfer may conflict with the general transfer restriction.

## What this screen cannot see
