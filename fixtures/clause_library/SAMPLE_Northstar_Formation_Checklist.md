# Formation Checklist — Northstar Holdings

> The order matters. An EIN needs the formation certificate; a bank account needs the EIN and an adopted operating agreement. Steps marked **gate** stop everything downstream until they clear. Filing fees are left blank on purpose — take them from the state's own current schedule rather than from here.

## Gates

- **Answer the open questions that block formation** (All) — Contributions, registered agent, and principal office all appear in the filing or in Schedule A. Filing without them means amending later, and an amendment is a public record of having got it wrong the first time.
- **Adopt the operating agreement and Schedule A** (All) — The certificate creates the entity; the operating agreement is what actually governs it. Until it is adopted, the state's default rules apply — which is precisely the outcome the structuring work exists to avoid.

## Why each step is where it is

**1. Answer the open questions that block formation** — Contributions, registered agent, and principal office all appear in the filing or in Schedule A. Filing without them means amending later, and an amendment is a public record of having got it wrong the first time.

**2. File the certificate of formation in Texas** — Creates the entity that equity in every operating entity; the cap table, the governance, and the investor rights sit here.

**3. Appoint a registered agent** — Required by the state, and the address where service of process lands. A missed service is a default judgment.

**4. Obtain an EIN** — Needed for the bank account and for any tax election. The application asks for the formation details, so it cannot run first.

**5. File the certificate of formation in Texas** — Creates the entity that title to the real property, and the mortgage.

**11. File the certificate of formation in Texas** — Creates the entity that employees, customer contracts, vendor contracts, insurance, and the day-to-day liability.

**12. Appoint a registered agent** — Required by the state, and the address where service of process lands. A missed service is a default judgment.

**13. Obtain an EIN** — Needed for the bank account and for any tax election. The application asks for the formation details, so it cannot run first.

**14. Adopt the operating agreement and Schedule A** — The certificate creates the entity; the operating agreement is what actually governs it. Until it is adopted, the state's default rules apply — which is precisely the outcome the structuring work exists to avoid.

**15. Open bank accounts, one per entity** — Separate entities that share a bank account are one entity to a court. This is the step that most often quietly undoes the whole structure.

**16. Execute the intercompany agreements the chart implies** — A lease from the property entity to the operating entity, a licence from the brand or rights entity, a services agreement where one entity employs the staff. A structure that exists only on the chart and never in a signed document is the structure a plaintiff pierces.

## Before any of this starts

- Nothing in this checklist is filed by Tessera OS. Every step is a human action, and the system records that it was taken rather than taking it.
- Fees and processing times come from each state's current schedule. They change, and a stale number in a checklist is worse than a blank one.
- Tessera is both advising and holding equity here. The dual-role disclosure should be signed before the operating agreement is adopted, not after.
