## 2. Fee and Fee Protection

**2.1 Introduction.** Consultant shall introduce Client to one or more prospective counterparties. An "Introduced Party" means any person or entity that Consultant first identifies to Client in writing, together with its affiliates, successors, and any entity under common control with it.

**2.2 Fee.** Client shall pay Consultant a fee equal to {fee_percentage} of the Transaction Value of any Transaction between Client and an Introduced Party. **2.3 Tail.** The fee is payable if a Transaction closes at any time during the Term or within twenty-four (24) months after termination or expiration of this Agreement.

**2.4 Payment.** The fee is earned on closing and payable in immediately available funds at closing, from closing proceeds where applicable.

## 4. Limitation of Liability

**4.1 Exclusion of Indirect Damages.** Neither party is liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, lost data, or loss of business opportunity, however caused and regardless of the theory of liability, even if advised of the possibility.

**4.2 Cap.** Each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client to Consultant under this Agreement.

**4.3 Carve-Outs.** Sections 4.1 and 4.2 do not apply to a party's fraud, wilful misconduct, or gross negligence, to a party's breach of its confidentiality obligations, or to any liability that cannot be limited or excluded as a matter of law.

**4.4 Allocation.** The parties agree that these limitations reflect an agreed allocation of risk, that the fees would be materially higher without them, and that they survive any failure of essential purpose of any limited remedy.

## 5. Indemnification

**5.1 Mutual Indemnity.** Each party (the "Indemnifying Party") shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party and its officers, members, and employees from and against third-party claims, and any resulting damages, losses, and reasonable costs (including reasonable attorneys' fees), to the extent arising from the Indemnifying Party's gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.

**5.2 Comparative Fault.** Neither party's indemnity obligation extends to any portion of a claim attributable to the negligence or wilful misconduct of the party seeking indemnity.

**5.3 Procedure.** The party seeking indemnity shall give prompt written notice of the claim, shall not settle it without the Indemnifying Party's written consent, and shall reasonably cooperate in the defence. The Indemnifying Party controls the defence and settlement, provided it may not settle in a way that imposes a non-monetary obligation on, or admits fault by, the indemnified party without that party's written consent.

**5.4 Relationship to Cap.** All indemnity obligations under this Section are subject to the limitation of liability in Section 7.
